Project two: DEMO Openbook2025

Problem: Small businesses and community hosts juggle event sign-ups and one-on-one appointments across messages and spreadsheets, and guests forget to show up.

What I did: Built an events and booking app in plain JavaScript. Guests browse a calendar, RSVP to events and book consultation slots. When an event is full, guests join a waitlist, and the next person moves in automatically if someone drops out. An admin page manages events, availability, bookings and a reminder queue. A demo clock lets you move time forward and watch the reminders go out. Everything is validated and escaped, and works in light and dark mode.

Project three: DEMO Budget-Tracker2025

Problem: People need a simple way to track their income and expenses without using a complicated budgeting system. It can be difficult to see how much money comes in, where money is being spent, and what the current balance is.

What I did: I built a React-based Budget Tracker that lets users add income and expenses with a description, amount, category, date, and currency. I added monthly filtering, income and expense summaries, a balance calculation, a six-month comparison chart, spending-by-category breakdown, and transaction deletion. I also used local storage so the data stays in the browser after the page is closed. I kept the design simple and responsive so it can work on both desktop and smaller screens.

Result: The finished app gives users a clear view of their finances in one place. They can quickly see their income, expenses, and balance, review spending patterns across six months, manage individual transactions, and keep their data stored in the browser.

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • React(JSX)
  • Vite
  • Node.js
